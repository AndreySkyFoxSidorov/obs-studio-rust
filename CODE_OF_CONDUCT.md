# Code of Conduct

SkyLive welcomes participation from people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project discussions. Critique ideas and code rather than people; respect differing viewpoints and privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and unwanted sexual attention are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. To report a concern, contact the repository owner privately using a contact method available on their GitHub profile; if none is available, request a private contact channel without posting sensitive details. The maintainer will handle reports with discretion where possible.

This policy applies to participation in SkyLive project spaces. The original OBS Studio project maintains its own [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/blob/master/COC.rst) and its own reporting channels.
